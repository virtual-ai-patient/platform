# Strategic Planning — History

This document tracks the evolution of the project's strategic plan.
Two revisions exist because the project goal changed in early March 2026.

| Revision | Goal | Date | Document |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rev 1** | Deliver a product to an industry partner (Third Opinion) | January 2026 | [strategic-planning-rev1.md](strategic-planning-rev1.md) |
| **Rev 2** | Deliver a startup proposal backed by a validated prototype | March 2026 | [strategic-planning-rev2.md](strategic-planning-rev2.md) |

## What changed and why

At project kickoff the team planned a 5-month feature roadmap aimed at
delivering a working platform to Third Opinion, an industry partner in
the medical education space. In early 2026 the partnership did not progress
to a signed agreement (NDA constraints on the university side), and the
team — in alignment with the course coordinator — adopted a new goal:

> Build a startup proposal for an AI-powered clinical training simulator,
> backed by a validated prototype with evidence from real users and a
> domain expert.

Rev 2 replaces the epic-driven, month-by-month delivery plan with a
three-phase hypothesis-driven plan: **Discovery → Prototype Iterations
(P1 / P2 / P3) → Proposal Finalisation**, with a pitch target in August 2026.

The current active plan is **[Revision 2](strategic-planning-rev2.md)**.
