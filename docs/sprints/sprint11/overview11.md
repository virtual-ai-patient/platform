# Meeting Overview & Action Points

---

## 1. Summary

The meeting focused on two main topics: **integrating expert feedback** (from Alexander) into the product direction, and **structuring the final retrospective presentation** to meet course requirements.

### Expert Feedback Integration (Alexander)

Based on the recent session with Alexander, the team identified three key focus areas:

1.  **Patient Persona Quality** — The LLM must behave like a real patient. Key behaviors include:
    - **Forgetfulness** — the patient forgets details over time.
    - **Deception** — the patient may lie or omit information.
    - **Slowness/Reluctance** — the patient may be slow to respond or reluctant to share.
    - **Consistency** — the clinical picture must remain consistent throughout the dialogue (no self-contradictions like "my leg hurts below the knee" followed by "I don't have a leg below the knee").

2.  **Clinical Case System** — A well-structured system is required, including:
    - Test cost and compatibility per case.
    - A realistic patient behavior model integrated with the case data.

3.  **Game Mechanics** — The simulator should function as a game, inspired by *Doctor House*:
    - Limited budget for tests (e.g., 5-10 tests per case).
    - Tests have individual costs.
    - The patient can mislead or confuse the student, creating a diagnostic puzzle.
    - The goal is to solve the case using limited resources.

### Strategic Direction & Retrospective Presentation

The team discussed how to structure the final presentation to align with the new direction while fulfilling course requirements.

- **New Goal:** Create an iterative prototype that supports a "startup proposal" by validating key hypotheses (patient role quality, clinical case system, game mechanics).
- **Retrospective Approach:** The presentation should explain:
    1.  The **original goal** (based on the Third Opinion client).
    2.  The **pivot** (triggered by the loss of the client and new expert insights).
    3.  The **new goal** (supporting a startup proposal with validated hypotheses).
    4.  How each **Practice Area** was addressed, linking actions to both the original and new goals.
- **Quality Attributes:** The mentor suggested that the team can justify their quality attribute choices by analyzing their cost and impact on the development timeline, which strengthens the startup proposal.

### Key Requirements for the Presentation

- **Practice Areas** must be explicitly covered and linked to the project's goals.
- The presentation must reflect **what the team actually built** (not just what they would have built).
- The retrospective should be **integrated with Practice Areas**, showing how each area was managed and how it contributed to the overall goal.
